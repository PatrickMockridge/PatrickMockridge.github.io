1Profile and method

Process safety taught me that a plausible number is not a safe number — so every result here is either proved or carries its limits. I was a process safety engineer from 2006 to 2015, chartered in 2013, and have worked independently since 2016 on protocol software, formal verification, and numerical systems. The method is the same in both fields: specify the failure mode before the architecture; prove what can be proved; contain what cannot; record provenance with every result. Where proof stops is stated in the limits.

The projects are instances of that method. DarkWow is containment: private coordination on a public chain, with authority held as an object capability rather than a public administrator. rnode-rust is continuity: a dissolved network’s node ported to Rust, with its language laws machine-checked at the model level and conformance-tested at the node. Azoth is provenance: chemical-engineering calculations where every number carries its source and verification state, and unsupported answers are refused. Mudra is verification in a familiar form: a spreadsheet calculation proved correct without revealing the sheet.
