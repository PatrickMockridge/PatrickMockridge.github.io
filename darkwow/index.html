Invention record

DarkWow

A Layer 1 design for private organisations on a public chain, where authority is represented as an object capability rather than asserted as an identity.

What
A Layer 1 design for private organisations on a public chain. Members coordinate, hold assets, and audit supply without a public administrator, a public member list, or a voter roll.
Why
An organisation needs privacy and accountability at the same time. A public chain gives auditability but exposes membership and strategy; a private chain gives confidentiality but asks members to trust an operator. DarkWow tries to get both without a single privileged key: authority is a name you hold, not an identity you assert, and supply is checked outside the proof. I could see that the basis of what DarkFi had could achieve this, but DarkFi had no interest in doing so.
The combination
ρ-calculus (2005) for authority as a name + Halo 2 (2020) for private state transitions + a native capability wallet that composes capability types from scanned state (2026) + a Pedersen supply audit outside the proof.

Method

Specify the failure mode before the architecture; prove what can be proved; contain what cannot; record provenance with every result. Where proof stops is stated in the limits.

1Design

  • Names are processes, and authority is a name. The ρ-calculus makes a name a quoted process (@P) and lets a process recover a process from a name (*x). Bundles give read/write facets, forwarders attenuate, and revocation is a language pattern. Authority is held, not asserted.
  • Authority can be held without a public roll. Governance is built from composable object-capability primitives — a purse, a box, an identity, an oracle, an attestation, a multisig — rather than a monolithic DAO or a privileged key. Holding the capability is what permits the action; the member list need not be public.
  • Competing histories resolve deterministically. Same-height blocks become uncles and share rewards; verification is stateless; there is no speculative execution and no operator-privileged reorg path.
  • Contracts describe their own interfaces. Each contract declares a TOML manifest in its deployment instructions — WASM exports, ZK circuits, state schema, capability requirements — so wallets and reviewers do not depend on an external registry; the on-chain manifest hash is pending.
  • The wallet bounds authority by what was scanned. Capability types are composed at scan time; key material stays in the account manager and never reaches the daemon.
  • Supply is checked outside the proof. Every block with native-token calls carries a Pedersen mass balance and a cumulative supply commitment; the check is pure Pedersen arithmetic, not a ZK verification. A block whose commitments violate the mass balance is rejected; the guarantee rests on Pedersen binding and a correct implementation. A protocol-level failure halts the chain rather than minting, and the repair is a protocol change rather than a quiet patch.

2Provenance

DarkWow is a fork of DarkFi. The zkVM, ZKAS circuit language, P2P stack, WASM runtime, and Halo2 backend are inherited. The fork-original work is the object-capability governance layer and genesis set, Uncle Merkle consensus, the TOML manifest/ABI, the capability wallet architecture, and three new ZK opcodes plus the supply audit. The code is AGPL-3.0.

The fork-era work was built by one primary developer with AI as a co-architect. The architecture is designed to contain what generated code can reach: capability boundaries, deterministic tests, and manifest-first integration. The formal record is in the obligation register.

4Limits

  • Nine genesis contracts are labelled proven; the 23 post-genesis contracts remain experimental, even where their circuits pass the manual instance-derivation audit.
  • Lean 4 models 32 zkVM opcodes and 10 gadgets; that layer is partial, with four assumptions. The 166/179 contract circuits have a manual instance-derivation audit, not Lean proofs. Value conservation and the Pedersen homomorphism are proved from the Pallas curve; the only surviving arithmetic assumption is pallasPrime. Eight axioms remain, and OBL-Z12 and other obligations are open.
  • The per-block Pedersen mass balance and cumulative supply chain are active; a block that violates the mass balance is rejected. External anchoring is partial: Caribina anchors are published but its verifier is not wired into the node; the Monero anchoring gadget has not been enabled; merge mining is live on the devnet/testnet, with mainnet TBD.
  • On-chain manifest hashing is pending.
  • The shipping wallet implements a subset of the ρ-calculus specification.
